# Student Performance Analysis – Business Insights

## Project Overview

This document presents the key findings obtained from analysing student academic performance across different demographic and educational factors.

The analysis focuses on Mathematics, Reading and Writing scores.

## 1. Gender-wise Performance

- Female students have an average total score of approximately 72.0.
- Male students have an average total score of approximately 72.1.
- The difference between the two groups is very small.

**Insight:** Academic performance is nearly similar across both gender groups in this dataset.

## 2. Ethnic Group Analysis

- Average scores across ethnic groups are relatively close.
- Reading scores are generally higher than Mathematics scores.
- Differences between male and female students within the groups are relatively small.

**Insight:** The dashboard shows relatively consistent academic performance across the ethnic groups.

## 3. Parent Education Analysis

- Students whose parents have a Master's degree show relatively high average scores in the displayed comparison.
- Students whose parents have a Bachelor's degree also show strong academic performance.
- Differences exist across parental education categories.

**Insight:** Parental education level may be associated with differences in student academic performance.

## 4. Test Preparation Analysis

- Students who completed test preparation have average scores of approximately 74.9–75.1.
- Students who did not complete test preparation have average scores of approximately 70.2–70.3.

**Insight:** Students who completed test preparation have higher average scores than students who did not complete it.

## 5. Parent Marital Status Analysis

- Students across different parental marital status categories have relatively similar average scores.
- The widowed category shows comparatively lower average scores in the displayed chart.

**Insight:** Academic performance varies slightly across parental marital status categories.

## 6. Sports Participation Analysis

- Students who sometimes participate in sports show average scores around 72.1–72.2.
- Students who regularly participate show average scores around 71.8.
- Students who never participate show average scores around 71.8–72.1.

**Insight:** The differences in average academic scores across sports participation groups are small.

## 7. Weekly Study Hours Analysis

- Students studying approximately 2.5 hours have average scores around 68.
- Students studying approximately 7.5 hours have average scores around 71.9–72.2.
- Students studying approximately 12 hours have average scores around 75.2–75.8.

**Insight:** Higher weekly study hours are associated with higher average academic scores in this dataset.

## 8. Subject-wise Performance

- Reading scores are generally higher than Mathematics scores.
- Writing scores fall between Mathematics and Reading scores in most displayed groups.
- Mathematics has comparatively lower average scores.

**Insight:** Mathematics may be an area where additional academic support could be explored.

## 9. Business Recommendations

Based on the observed patterns:

1. Encourage students to participate in structured test preparation activities.
2. Identify students who may benefit from additional Mathematics support.
3. Encourage consistent study habits.
4. Monitor academic performance across different student groups.
5. Provide additional learning support based on individual student performance.

## 10. Conclusion

The analysis highlights patterns between academic performance and factors such as test preparation, weekly study hours, parental education and demographic groups.

Test preparation and weekly study hours show noticeable differences in average scores in the dashboard.

These findings can help educators identify areas for further investigation and develop appropriate academic support strategies.

Note: These observations describe associations in the dataset and do not establish that any particular factor directly causes higher or lower academic performance.
